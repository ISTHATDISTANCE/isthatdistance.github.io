I am currently a first-year PhD student in Computer Science (CS) at Georgia Tech, working with Prof. [Yalong Yang](https://ivi.cc.gatech.edu/pi.html) on Human-Computer Interaction (**HCI**) problems in Augmented/Virtual Reality (**AR/VR**). I obtained [Master's degree in HCI](https://mshci.gatech.edu/) from Georgia Tech and Bachelor's degree in CS from both University of Michigan (UM) and Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). During my undergraduate time at UM, I worked with Prof. <span "color:blue">[Anhong Guo](guoanhong.com)</span> on Accessibility and Assistive Technologies.

I am interested in designing **immersive experiences for storytelling**. As AR/VR ecology is developing, we want this promising technology to be more deeply leveraged in a variety of storytelling environments, from people's daily entertainment to professional information communications. To push this trend forward, I am currently focusing on the <u>visual and interaction design for daily news storytelling</u>. I am really excited to work with my cohort to make content creation and consumption more enjoying in AR/VR.
