I design and study **immersive experiences for storytelling**—exploring how people create, navigate, and understand stories in augmented and virtual reality.

At Georgia Tech, I work with [Prof. Yalong Yang](https://ivi.cc.gatech.edu/pi.html) on human–computer interaction in AR/VR. My research brings together visual and interaction design to make immersive storytelling more engaging and accessible, from everyday news to professional communication.

Previously, I worked with [Prof. Anhong Guo](https://guoanhong.com) at the University of Michigan on accessibility and assistive technologies.
